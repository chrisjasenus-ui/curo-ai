# Curo AI - AI Appointment Scheduling for Healthcare

Curo AI is a healthcare-focused AI voice automation platform designed to improve patient access and streamline appointment management through intelligent automation.

## AI Appointment Scheduling for Healthcare

Modern healthcare providers face increasing pressure to manage high volumes of appointment requests, cancellations, and rescheduling tasks. Traditional manual scheduling systems often lead to delays, missed calls, and inefficient front-desk operations.

Curo AI helps solve these challenges with **AI-powered appointment scheduling for healthcare organizations**.

- 24/7 automated appointment booking

- Lower administrative workload

## How It Works

Curo AI integrates with healthcare workflows to automate scheduling through voice and conversational AI systems.

The system can:
- Understand patient requests in natural language  
- Check availability in real-time systems  
- Book or reschedule appointments instantly  
- Route complex cases to human staff when needed
